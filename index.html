A PS5 DualSense as a push-to-talk voice input device for macOS. The trigger toggles dictation, the light bar breathes while you speak, haptics confirm. ~200 lines of Swift in your menu bar.

✕ Quick start

Clone, build, pull the trigger.

A game-controller icon appears in the menu bar. Plug in the DualSense — or pair over Bluetooth with a long press on PS + Create — then press R2, dictate, press R2 again.

Requires macOS 11.3+, Swift 5.9+, and OpenWhispr on Option+` in tap mode.
Autostart & Accessibility setup: docs/install.md

$ git clone https://github.com/SunkyYang/vibe-controller.git
$ cd vibe-controller
$ swift build -c release
$ ./.build/release/VibeController
○ From trigger to text

The controller was already in arm’s reach.

OpenWhispr already handles audio, transcription, and text injection. All that was missing was a way to turn a perfectly good analog trigger into the shortcut it listens for. That’s the whole app — the light show comes free.

Pull

R2 goes down

A soft haptic tick confirms. The light bar starts breathing blue and rises with your voice level.

→
Speak

OpenWhispr listens

The app fires the dictation shortcut. Whisper transcribes locally — the controller is just the talk button.

→
Land

Text at your cursor

Release R2. A double-tap haptic signs off and the words are injected wherever you were typing.

□ The fun parts

Built because it’s delightful, kept because it works.

Voice-reactive light bar

Breathes while recording, flares with your voice level. Amber while L2 is held, so you always know which layer you’re on.

Haptic vocabulary

Distinct rumble patterns for start, stop, mode changes, and long presses. You stop looking at the screen surprisingly fast.

Claude Code on the light bar

Optional state mirroring: the bar shows whether Claude is thinking, waiting on you, or done.

Adaptive trigger weapon mode

R2 can push back with real trigger resistance (USB only). Entirely unnecessary. Entirely worth it.

Sticks are a mouse

Left stick moves the cursor, right stick scrolls, □ clicks and drags. Couch-driving your Mac is a real workflow.

Menu bar only

No Dock icon, no window. Runs as a LaunchAgent at login, over USB or Bluetooth. Quit from the menu bar.
